ABOUT

Stapleford Watches is an independent, UK-based specialist dedicated to sourcing and curating unique, historically significant timepieces across both modern and vintage collections. Whether you are purchasing your first luxury watch or adding to an established collection, we aim to offer a distinctive selection from exceptional brands.

AUTHENTICITY

Each watch is carefully inspected in-house before sale to verify its authenticity, accuracy and condition. A certificate from The Watch Register can be made available upon request.

SERVICES

We offer a range of services including watch restoration, servicing, polishing and valuations. If you have a specific request, please get in touch using the contact details provided.

WARRANTY

We provide a 12-month warranty on all watches, in conjunction with any remaining manufacturer’s warranty where applicable.

Please note that our warranty covers mechanical defects only and does not cover damage caused by the buyer. For vintage models, we cannot guarantee waterproofness or precise timekeeping unless the watch has been serviced and such requests have been approved by our watchmaker.

PAYMENT

We accept bank transfers, PayPal and credit cards through our platform.

DELIVERY

UK: We offer complimentary next-day delivery within the UK. We aim to dispatch all orders on the same day that cleared funds are received. Parcels will require a signature upon delivery, and tracking information will be provided on the date of dispatch.

Collection is also available upon request. Please contact us for more information.

International: Please contact us for a shipping quote. International shipments may be subject to customs duties, taxes and import fees levied by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the buyer.

RETURNS

We are confident that you will love your new timepiece. However, if for any reason you are not satisfied with your purchase, please contact us within 14 days of receipt.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused and in the same condition in which you received it, including the original packaging and any additional accessories where applicable.

When returning your item, please ensure that you use a tracked and fully insured delivery method. Returns are subject to inspection upon receipt.

To process a return, please contact us via email or phone/WhatsApp: ben@staplefordwatches.co.uk or +44 7438 196047.
